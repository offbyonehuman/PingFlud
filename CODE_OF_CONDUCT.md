# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Ping Flud aims to be a welcoming, practical project for people interested in Windows and network diagnostics. Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- focus on the technical issue rather than the person;
- assume good faith while remaining open to correction;
- protect private network details, credentials, and customer information; and
- accept responsibility for the impact of their words and actions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, and sharing sensitive network data without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces where the community is represented. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not include passwords, tokens, real private-network addresses, scan results, or customer data. Security vulnerabilities must be reported according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.
